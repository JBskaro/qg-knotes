# Code of conduct

QG Knotes is made by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We want working on it to be
pleasant and productive for everyone, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Where it applies

In every project space: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and wherever a person
represents the project in public, for example when writing on its behalf.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people. Criticism is about the work and comes with reasons.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Accept responsibility for your mistakes, apologize to those affected and learn from them.
- Accept that the maintainer makes the final decision about what goes into the project.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, threats, sexualized language or images, discriminatory jokes or remarks.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or a phone number, without their permission.
- Spam, trolling and repeated off-topic messages.
- Any other behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Who enforces these rules

The maintainer of the project enforces this code. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues
and other contributions that break it, and explains the reason when that is appropriate.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behavior that breaks these rules, write to the maintainer privately through the contact
on the maintainer's GitHub profile, https://github.com/JBskaro. Describe what happened, where and when, with links
if you have them. You will get an answer, and the report stays confidential: the name of the person who reported is
not disclosed without their consent.

## Consequences

The maintainer decides on a consequence after hearing the people involved:

1. **Correction.** A private note explaining what was wrong; a public apology may be asked for.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behavior; no interaction with the people involved for a
   set time.
3. **Temporary ban.** No interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban.** No interaction with the project at all, for a pattern of violations or a single serious one,
   such as harassment or threats.

## Attribution

These rules follow the structure of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 3.0.
